Research

Berkeley AI Research Lab Jan 2025 – present

  • Studied how weight tying affects token embeddings across 5+ LLMs using geometric subspace and gradient-scaling experiments (see publications).

Lawrence Berkeley National Lab Jan 2026 – present

  • Up to 30% lower RMSE than stationary baselines on chirp, topography, and X-ray scattering data. Characterized how stationary kernels miscalibrate uncertainty and mislead acquisition in Bayesian experimental design.
